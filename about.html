About

Who We Are

SORC is an independent research collaborative conducting systematic reviews and studies using public data. We study questions in medicine and health that have received limited research attention. Junior researchers contribute throughout each project, with advice from experienced researchers and clinicians where needed. Read our authorship and collaboration standards.

Contact us at info@sorc.ca

01

Question selection

Our researchers choose clinical and research methods questions that existing studies have not adequately answered.

02

Open data methods

We use published research and other public data sources, with methods designed so others can check how we reached our findings.

03

Expert collaboration

Clinicians and senior researchers advise on projects in their fields. SORC members carry out the study design, analysis and writing.
