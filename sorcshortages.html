SORCShortages

SORCShortages: A View of Canadian Drug Shortage and Discontinuation Reports

Search and compare Canadian drug shortage and discontinuation reports.

SORCShortages uses dated snapshots from Health Product Shortages Canada. Each report is kept as one episode. The dataset is refreshed monthly on the first of the month.

Before using the data
  • Reports can be anticipated, avoided or revised. A report does not confirm a shortage occurred or establish local stock levels or patient impact.
  • Durations for ongoing actual shortages end at the snapshot date. Estimated end dates do not confirm resolution.
  • SORCShortages is not affiliated with Health Canada or Health Product Shortages Canada. It is intended for research, not clinical or operational decisions.

Methodology
Data and Episode Construction

Source. The dashboard shows the report count and date of the latest source snapshot.

Episode construction. Each report is one episode. Start dates use the actual date when available, otherwise the anticipated date. Active counts include only reports labelled as actual shortages whose dates cover the snapshot. Anticipated reports do not become actual shortages simply because their start date has passed. Durations are calculated for resolved and actual shortages with usable dates. Ongoing durations end at the snapshot. Anticipated, avoided and discontinuation reports, and reports with future starts, have no observed shortage duration. Actual end dates after the snapshot are retained and flagged. Estimated end dates are not treated as observed resolution.

Report types. Shortages and discontinuations follow the source classifications and are counted separately.

Tier 3. Tier 3 labels come directly from the source.

Pattern series. The chart begins in March 2017, when mandatory reporting began. Earlier records remain searchable. The chart includes valid actual, anticipated and avoided reporting intervals that overlap this period, including those that began earlier. It describes reporting rather than confirmed patient shortages. No interval extends beyond its source observation date. The duration median and histogram combine completed and ongoing observations. They do not estimate time to resolution.

Quality handling. Original dates are retained in the records or original snapshot. Invalid dates, ends before starts and actual end dates after the snapshot are flagged. The dashboard shows flag counts. Avoidance before an anticipated start is an expected reporting pattern. Missing optional fields remain missing.

Update schedule. A new snapshot is collected on the first of each month.

Research downloads. Download CSV records, the full JSON, the source audit or the data guide. The dashboard also links to its original compressed snapshot. Snapshots have been retained since version 1.1 and do not capture every source revision. Keep the dated snapshot and its hash with your analysis.
