About the counts and dates

Active counts use reports labelled as actual shortages whose dates cover the data date. Tier 3 counts include flagged shortage reports across all statuses.

The median includes completed shortages and ongoing observations. Ongoing durations end at the last observation. The median does not estimate time to resolution.
